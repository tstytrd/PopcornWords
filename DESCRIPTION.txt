A fun, speech-powered Android app for kids to practice the 220 Dolch sight words. Hold the mic button, say the word, and get instant feedback! Features mastery loops, tricky word tracking, grade-level categories (Pre-Primer through 3rd Grade), per-word toggle controls, and a parent dashboard behind a math gate. Built with Kotlin, Jetpack Compose, Room, and Android SpeechRecognizer. 🍿⭐
