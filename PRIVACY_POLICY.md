# Privacy Policy for Popcorn Words

**Last Updated:** November 17, 2026

Popcorn Words ("we," "our," or "the app") is committed to protecting the privacy of children, parents, and educators. This Privacy Policy explains our privacy practices regarding the Popcorn Words Android mobile application.

---

### 1. COPPA and Children's Online Privacy Protection
Popcorn Words is designed specifically for children from early childhood through elementary grades (ages 4–11). We strictly adhere to the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR-K for children), and Google Play's Families Policy Requirements.

**We do NOT collect, transmit, store, sell, or share any personal information from children or any other users.**

---

### 2. Information We Do NOT Collect
- **No Personal Information:** We do not collect names, email addresses, physical addresses, phone numbers, ages, birthdays, or unique online identifiers.
- **No User Accounts:** The app does not require account creation, logins, passwords, or social media links.
- **No Tracking or Advertising:** Popcorn Words is completely ad-free. We do not use third-party analytics, behavioral tracking, ad networks, or device fingerprinting.
- **No In-App Purchases:** The app contains no microtransactions, subscriptions, or financial transactions.

---

### 3. Microphone & Audio Permission (Speech Recognition)
Popcorn Words requests permission to access the device's microphone (`android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO`) solely to enable speech recognition during sight word practice sessions:

- **Local & System Processing:** Spoken audio is evaluated using Android's native speech recognition framework (`android.speech.SpeechRecognizer`).
- **No Audio Recording or Storage:** We do **not** record, save, store, or transmit your or your child's voice recordings to any private or external server.
- Audio data is discarded immediately after recognition evaluation completes.

---

### 4. Local Data Storage on Device
All app progress data is saved **locally on your device** using an internal SQLite database (Room):
- Word mastery progress (correct answer count, practice attempts)
- Custom word lists created or imported by parents
- Enabled/disabled word settings
- Tricky word markers

This data never leaves your device. If you uninstall the application or clear app data, this locally stored information is permanently deleted.

---

### 5. Parental Controls (Parent Gate)
To protect children and provide parent-guided customization, the app includes a Parental Gate (a randomized math verification problem) before entering Parent Mode. This ensures that word list modifications and file imports remain under parental supervision.

---

### 6. Third-Party Services
Popcorn Words does not integrate third-party SDKs, marketing trackers, cloud analytics, or advertising libraries.

---

We may periodically update this Privacy Policy. Any updates will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
