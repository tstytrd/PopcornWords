🛡️ Privacy Commitment

Last Updated: November 17, 2026

Popcorn Words is built specifically for early readers (ages 4 to 11). We believe children's educational apps should be joyful, distraction-free, and uncompromising when it comes to privacy.

We strictly follow the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), GDPR-K, and Google Play's Families Policy. We do NOT collect, sell, or share personal information from children or adults.

🎙️ Microphone & Speech Recognition

Popcorn Words requests access to your device's microphone (android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO) solely to allow children to practice reading sight words out loud.

  • On-Device Evaluation: Speech audio is processed directly through Android's native speech recognition framework (SpeechRecognizer).
  • No Audio Storage: Audio is never recorded, saved to a file, or uploaded to any private server.
  • Instant Deletion: The audio snippet is discarded the instant speech recognition matching is complete.

💾 How Data is Stored (Local Only)

All learning progress stays exclusively on your physical phone or tablet in a local SQLite/Room database:

  • Practice scores (how many times a word was practiced or read correctly)
  • "Tricky Words" bookmarks
  • Parent-customized lists and toggles

This data never connects to the internet or any cloud database. If you uninstall the app or clear app data in Android settings, all saved progress is wiped clean from your device.

🚸 Parental Gate

To ensure young children stay focused on learning and cannot accidentally alter custom settings, all configuration controls (adding words, importing files, or toggling grade decks) are protected behind a Parental Gate requiring the answer to a randomized math question.

💻 Open Source & Transparency

Popcorn Words is free and open-source software. Anyone — parents, educators, and software engineers — can inspect our complete source code to independently verify that no tracking, analytics, or data extraction code exists in the app.
